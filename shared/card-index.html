Backdoors & Breaches card list

B&B Shuffle · every card, deck by deck ← Card Catalogue

This is the complete card list for the decks loaded by B&B Shuffle, the free, unofficial online companion for Backdoors & Breaches. Cards are grouped by deck and then by type — initial compromise, pivot and escalate, C2 and exfiltration, persistence, procedure, inject and consultant. The interactive catalogue shows the card faces, card text, TOOLS and DETECTION sections; this page is the text index search engines and screen readers can follow. Backdoors & Breaches is published by Black Hills Information Security and Antisyphon Training; this project is not affiliated with either.
